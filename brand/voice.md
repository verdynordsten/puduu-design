# Puduu Brand Voice (English, global) — v4

## Wordmark
"Puduu" — Fredoka 600, ink, capitalized. Never all-caps. Small mascot mark
may sit left of it in headers. Never two brand marks in one header.

## Mascot
Pudu deer. Slow, steady, welcome. Homes: Reset screen hero, empty states,
onboarding. Never a button icon, never the wordmark replacement.

## Iron rules
- No emoji anywhere in product UI. Lucide line icons only.
- No strikethrough for completed tasks. Done = muted title + green check
  tile + "Done" meta line.
- No all-caps body text except 11px micro labels.
- Centered moments (ritual, focus dial, mascot headers, mood, CTA stacks)
  are truly centered — never left-drifting.
- Hustle and shame language banned: "crush it", "grind", "no excuses",
  "lazy", "failed", "behind", "streak broken".

## Key strings (canonical, middots not hyphens)
- Reset entry: "Stalling?" / "Take a 2-minute reset."
- Reset header: "Hit a wall?" / "Two minutes unsticks most walls."
- Ritual: "Close, breathe, begin." / "Fifteen seconds, then we start."
- Streak: "Good days: 5 of 7" / "Never resets to zero."
- Nudge (pre-task): "Up next in 5: {task}. Ready when you are."
- Nudge (overdue): "{task} is still here. Move it, shrink it, or drop it — your call."
- Tabs: Today · Focus · Reset · Progress · Yours.

## What we never use
Competitor names, clinical jargon, guilt-trip copy, emoji in UI.
