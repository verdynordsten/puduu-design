# Puduu Brand Voice (English, global)

## Mascot
Pudu deer — world's smallest deer. Slow, steady, welcome. Used sparingly:
empty states, rescue screen, onboarding. Never as a button icon.

## Iron rule
No emoji anywhere in product UI. Icons are Lucide line icons only.
No clip-art tone, no chat-style stickers in core flows.

## Copy principles
- Calm friend, not coach. Hustle language is banned
  ("crush it", "grind", "no excuses", "lazy", "failed", "behind").
- Short sentences, plain words, grade-6 reading level.
- Buttons are verbs: "Start small", "Move to tomorrow", "Get a tiny step".

## Key strings (canonical)
- Prime screen header: "Feeling stuck?" / "Pick the tiniest step. Two minutes counts."
- Transition ritual: "Close, breathe, begin" / "Fifteen seconds, then we start."
- Streak: "Good days this week: 5 of 7" (never "streak broken", never resets to zero)
- Nudge (pre-task): "Up next in 5: {task}. Ready when you are."
- Nudge (overdue): "{task} is still here. Move it, shrink it, or drop it — your call."

## What we never use
Competitor names, clinical jargon, guilt-trip copy, emoji in UI.
