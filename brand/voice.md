# Puduu Brand Voice (English, global)

## Mascot
Pudu deer — world's smallest deer. Slow, steady, cute. Narrative: "Small steps count."

## Copy principles
- Friend, not coach. No hustle language ("crush it", "grind", "no excuses" = banned).
- No shame words: never "failed", "missed", "lazy", "behind". Use "paused", "moved", "later".
- Short sentences. Grade-6 reading level. Contractions welcome ("Let's", "You've").
- Buttons are verbs: "Start small", "Move to tomorrow", "Rescue me".

## Key strings (canonical)
- Freeze rescue entry: "I'm Stuck" / subtitle "Frozen? Let's thaw with one tiny step."
- Rescue options header: "Pick the tiniest one"
- Transition ritual: "Close → Breathe → Begin" / "15 seconds. Then we start."
- Streak: "Good days this week: 5/7 🎉" (never "streak broken")
- Nudge (pre-task): "Up next in 5: {task}. No rush — it's ready when you are."
- Nudge (overdue): "{task} is still here. Move it, shrink it, or drop it — your call."

## What we never say
Tiimo/AI Co-Planner/competitor names, clinical jargon, guilt-trip copy.
